About

Hi, I'm Asmi.

A Computer Science student at MIT Bangalore who got into finance in the most practical way possible, by actually running a business.

That experience showed me how confusing and exhausting managing money can feel when you're handling everything on your own. You care deeply about your business, but the financial side often feels fragmented, overwhelming, and difficult to fully understand.

It made me start asking a question I couldn't stop thinking about:

Why is financial clarity still so inaccessible for small business owners?

Most tools are either too expensive, too complicated, or designed for companies that already have finance teams. Most small businesses have none of that. They just have owners trying to make good decisions with limited visibility.

That question pulled me into fintech.

Along the way, I also became deeply interested in markets themselves. The structure underneath price movements, the logic beneath apparent chaos, and the patterns that begin to emerge when you look closely enough.

So I started building.

Each project taught me something new about systems, markets, and the way money actually moves.

Right now, I'm building Ledgr, a financial tool designed for small business owners who are exceptional at what they do but shouldn't have to struggle to understand their own finances.

Originally from Mumbai, currently building in Bangalore, and still very early in the journey.

Startup · In Progress
Ledgr

Ledgr is a fintech-focused project aimed at helping small business owners better understand and manage their finances through simple, modern digital tools.

The goal behind Ledgr is to make financial clarity more accessible by simplifying things like cash flow tracking, operational visibility, and day-to-day financial decision making for businesses that often don't have dedicated finance teams or expensive software.

At its core, Ledgr is built around the idea that business owners should be able to focus on running their business without constantly feeling overwhelmed by the financial side of it.
